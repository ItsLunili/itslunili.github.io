Privacy Policy

Better TTS
Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 25, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Better TTS (“the Bot”, “Better TTS”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Discord bot, website, and related services.

Better TTS is a Discord bot and software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform that provides text-to-speech functionality, server management features, and optional premium subscription services.

By using Better TTS, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Data Controller

Better TTS is responsible for processing personal information collected through our services.

For privacy-related questions or requests, you may contact us using the contact information provided at the end of this Privacy Policy.

3. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to operate and improve Better TTS.

Discord Information

When you use Better TTS, we may collect and store:

  • Discord User ID
  • Discord Server (Guild) ID
  • Discord Channel ID
  • Discord Username or display information
  • Voice channel information necessary for text-to-speech functionality

We do not collect Discord passwords or access credentials.

User Preferences

To provide customizable features, we may store:

  • Selected text-to-speech voice
  • Speech rate
  • Pitch settings
  • Volume settings
  • Pronunciation overrides
  • Other preferences configured by users

Server Configuration Data

For server administrators, we may store:

  • Server settings
  • Text-to-speech configuration
  • Premium activation information
  • Feature settings
  • Custom server preferences

Message Content

Better TTS processes message content only when necessary to provide text-to-speech functionality.

Messages may be temporarily processed to:

  • Convert text into speech
  • Apply pronunciation settings
  • Generate the requested audio output

We do not intentionally store message content after processing unless required for debugging, security, abuse prevention, or a feature that explicitly requires storage.

Subscription and Payment Information

Premium subscriptions are processed through Stripe.

We may store:

  • Stripe Customer ID
  • Stripe Subscription ID
  • Subscription status
  • Subscription plan information
  • Premium activation information

We do not store:

  • Full credit card numbers
  • Banking information
  • Payment authentication details

Payment information is handled by Stripe according to their own privacy policy.

Website Information

When visiting our website, we may collect limited technical information, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Basic analytics information
  • Cookies required for website functionality

4. How We Use Collected Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide text-to-speech functionality
  • Save user and server preferences
  • Manage Premium subscriptions
  • Process payments
  • Improve and maintain Better TTS
  • Detect abuse and security issues
  • Provide customer support
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users located in the European Union or United Kingdom, we process personal information under applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Our legal bases for processing may include:

  • Providing the service you requested
  • Your consent
  • Legitimate interests such as maintaining security and improving our services
  • Compliance with legal obligations

You may withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis for processing. This does not affect processing that occurred before consent was withdrawn.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

We may share information only when necessary with trusted third parties, including:

Service Providers

We may share limited information with providers that help us operate Better TTS, such as:

  • Discord
  • Stripe
  • Hosting providers
  • Database providers
  • Infrastructure providers

These services only receive information necessary to provide their services.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required to:

  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Respond to lawful requests
  • Protect our rights or property
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or security threats

7. Third-Party Services

Better TTS relies on third-party platforms, including Discord and Stripe.

These services have their own privacy policies and practices. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide Better TTS, maintain subscriptions, prevent abuse, and comply with legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, we may delete or anonymize it.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect stored information.

However, no online service can guarantee complete security. While we work to protect your information, you acknowledge that internet-based services carry unavoidable security risks.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • Accessing your personal information
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information
  • Requesting deletion of your information
  • Requesting restriction of processing
  • Objecting to certain processing activities
  • Withdrawing consent where applicable

To make a request, contact us using the information below.

11. Children’s Privacy

Better TTS is not intended for users below the minimum age required by Discord’s Terms of Service.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from users who are not permitted to use Discord.

If we become aware that prohibited information has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to remove it.

12. International Data Processing

Your information may be processed or stored in countries outside your country of residence.

By using Better TTS, you acknowledge that your information may be transferred where necessary to operate the service.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Better TTS, our data practices, or legal requirements.

The updated version will be posted on our website with a revised effective date.

14. Complaints

If you have concerns about how your information is handled, please contact us first so we can attempt to resolve the issue.

Depending on your location, you may also have the right to contact your local data protection authority.
